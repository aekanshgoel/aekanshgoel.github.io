I used to pack evenings the same way I packed days: one more errand, one more message, one more thing to optimize. A slower night made it obvious that I had been treating rest like a leftover.

Margin is not empty. It is the space where judgment comes back.

I still like a full weekend. Tennis or pickleball, a good meal, time outside. The difference is leaving a little unscheduled on purpose.
