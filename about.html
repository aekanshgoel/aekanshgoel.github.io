About

Atlanta. Tennis on weekends. A good meal when I can find one. And a job that is basically organized curiosity.

I work in manufacturing engineering. I like watching how things actually get done. Then I try to make the next round less annoying.

I notice friction. I ask too many questions. If a process only works because someone is being a hero, I don’t trust it.

That same itch follows me around. I like learning something I can use this week. I write things down so they don’t vanish. I leave a little space in the calendar on purpose.

The rest of my life is not a productivity system. I play pickleball. I walk. I stay out for a drink. I watch movies. I sit in the sauna until my brain gets quiet again.

Travel is my reset button. New city, new food, no need to be an expert. I like being a beginner somewhere.

This site is where I practice saying things simply. Work. Getting better. The fun parts too. Say hi if you want. The links below work.

A few things I care about

  • Work that doesn’t fight the people doing it
  • Learning in public, a little at a time
  • Tennis, pickleball, and being outside
  • Restaurants, drinks, and unhurried nights
  • Travel, movies, and a little quiet
